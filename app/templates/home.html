Adverse-event terms are coded against a vocabulary written for this demonstration — MedDRA and WHODrug are licensed dictionaries and are not used.

Deliberately not built

  • Authentication and access control. The role dashboards select and reorder what each reader sees; they restrict nothing, and every screen stays reachable from every one of them.
  • Licensed dictionaries. MedDRA and WHODrug are commercial and under subscription.
  • Live EDC / ABDM exchange. FHIR R4 resource shapes exist; a counterparty system does not.
  • SDTM beyond DM. One domain demonstrates the mapping.

Each is a scoping decision with a stated reason, not an omission.
